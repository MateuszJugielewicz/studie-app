# Refund & Cancellation Policy

Version 2026-09-25

Each studio chooses one of three cancellation policies. The policy is shown on the studio profile and at checkout.

## If the artist cancels
- **Flexible:** full refund up to 24 hours before the session. No refund after that.
- **Moderate:** full refund up to 72 hours before the session, and 50% up to 24 hours before. No refund after that.
- **Strict:** 50% refund up to 7 days before the session. No refund after that.

Refunds go back to the card or Apple Pay you paid with, and usually arrive within 5–10 business days.

## If the studio cancels or declines
The artist always gets a **full refund**. For requests the studio declines, the card hold is released and nothing is charged. Studios that cancel often may be suspended.

## Requests the studio doesn't answer
If a studio doesn't answer a booking request before the session starts, the request expires and the card hold is released.

## Deposits
If a studio requires a deposit, the refund rules apply to the amount you have paid. The remaining balance is only charged after a completed session.

## Cash bookings
When you choose to pay cash at the studio, nothing is charged in the app, so there is nothing to refund if the booking is cancelled. Please cancel in the app as early as you can. Studios may stop accepting cash from artists who repeatedly don't show up.

## No-shows and problems
If the session didn't happen as booked (for example, the studio was closed or double-booked, or the equipment described wasn't there), report a problem on the booking within 48 hours. EasySesh reviews the case and can issue a full or partial refund. Artists who don't show up are not refunded under the studio's policy.

## Changing a booking
You can move a confirmed booking to another free slot with the same length for free, as long as the session hasn't started.
