# Studio Agreement

Version 2026-09-25 · Between [COMPANY NAME] ("EasySesh") and the business listing a studio ("Studio"). This agreement comes in addition to the Terms & Conditions.

## 1. Application and approval
- The Studio applies with correct and complete information: photos of the actual rooms, its address, prices, equipment and opening hours.
- EasySesh reviews each application and may approve, reject or ask for changes at its own discretion. Studio tools (bookings, calendar, payouts and messaging with artists) are only available after approval.
- EasySesh may verify the Studio's identity, business registration and bank account, including through Stripe.

## 2. Listing
- The Studio keeps its listing, prices, calendar and opening hours up to date, and blocks times it can't offer.
- Photos and descriptions must match reality. Misleading listings may be suspended.

## 3. Platform fee: 10%
EasySesh charges a **platform fee of 10%** of the price of every booking made through EasySesh (session price including add-ons, excluding refunded amounts).
- **Card payments:** EasySesh collects the payment from the artist and pays the Studio 90% via Stripe Connect, normally 2 days after the completed session.
- **Cash payments:** the artist pays the Studio directly. The 10% fee becomes payable to EasySesh when the session is completed. EasySesh deducts it from the Studio's next card payouts. Any balance not covered within 30 days is invoiced, with payment due within 14 days.
- The Studio must mark cash as received in the app. If it doesn't, cash is treated as received 3 days after the session, unless the Studio has reported a no-show or problem.
- Fees are exclusive of VAT, which is added where applicable. The Studio is responsible for its own taxes and for reporting its income, including cash income.

## 4. Payouts
Payouts require a completed Stripe Connect onboarding. EasySesh may withhold payouts that are subject to a dispute, a suspected fraud or a chargeback until the matter is resolved.

## 5. Cancellations by the Studio
If the Studio cancels, the artist is refunded in full. Repeated cancellations or no-shows by the Studio may lead to suspension. Refunds on card bookings reduce the Studio's payout accordingly.

## 6. Conduct and safety
The Studio complies with applicable law, including health and safety rules, fire safety, insurance and accessibility statements. It treats artists respectfully and must not discriminate.

## 7. Circumvention
The Studio must not ask artists it met through EasySesh to book or pay outside EasySesh in order to avoid the platform fee. EasySesh may charge the fee it lost and suspend the Studio.

## 8. Data protection
The Studio receives artists' personal data (name, profile and booking details) only to deliver the booking. It processes that data as an independent controller in compliance with the GDPR, and must not use it for marketing without the artist's consent.

## 9. Term and termination
Either party may end this agreement at any time. Obligations for bookings already made, and fees already owed, continue. EasySesh may suspend a studio with immediate effect for serious breach.

## 10. Liability
The Studio is responsible for the sessions it delivers, its premises and its equipment, and holds appropriate liability insurance.
