# Community Guidelines

Version 2026-09-25

Sonora is for making music. To keep it safe and useful:

## Be real
- Use your real artist or studio name. Photos must show the actual studio.
- Only review sessions you actually had. Don't post fake reviews and don't pay for reviews.

## Be respectful
- No harassment, hate speech, threats or sexual content.
- Chat is for arranging and discussing bookings. It isn't a social network.

## Keep bookings on Sonora
- Don't ask to move a booking you arranged on Sonora outside the platform to avoid fees. Cash bookings are fine when they are made through the app.
- Never share passwords or payment details in chat.

## Reporting
Tap **Report** on a studio, review, message or booking. Our moderation team reviews every report, usually within 24 hours, and may hide content or suspend accounts. We don't tell the reported person who reported them.

Sonora doesn't tolerate retaliation against people who report in good faith.
