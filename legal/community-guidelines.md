# Community Guidelines

Version 2026-09-26

EasySesh is for making music. To keep it safe and useful:

## Be real
- Use your real artist or studio name. Photos must show the actual studio.
- Only review sessions you actually had. Don't post fake reviews and don't pay for reviews.
- Studios rate artists and artists rate studios. Be fair and honest. If you think a rating is unfair, you can dispute it and our team will look into it.
- Use check-in honestly: only check in when you are actually at the studio.

## Be respectful
- No harassment, hate speech, threats or sexual content.
- Chat is for arranging and discussing bookings. It isn't a social network.

## Keep bookings on EasySesh
- Don't ask to move a booking you arranged on EasySesh outside the platform to avoid fees. Cash bookings are fine when they are made through the app.
- Never share passwords or payment details in chat.

## Reporting
Tap **Report** on a studio, review, message or booking. Our moderation team reviews every report, usually within 24 hours, and may hide content or suspend accounts. We don't tell the reported person who reported them.

EasySesh doesn't tolerate retaliation against people who report in good faith.

## What happens if the rules are broken
Depending on how serious it is, our team can:
- send you a **warning**, which you see in the app;
- **suspend** your account or studio for a period (for example 1, 3 or 7 days), after which it is lifted automatically;
- **ban** your account, for a period or permanently.

We keep a record of warnings, suspensions and reports so we can act consistently. If you think we made a mistake, contact support.
